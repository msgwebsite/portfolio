About Me

Data Science · Bayesian Statistics · Network Science · INSPIRE Scholar

Hi, I'm Maitreya! I'm a BS-MS Data Science student at IISER Thiruvananthapuram, where I spend most of my time on Bayesian statistics, probabilistic modeling, and network science. I like building principled models for messy, high-dimensional data — especially single-cell genomics like scRNA-seq and scATAC-seq.

Lately I've been most excited about Bayesian inference, unsupervised feature selection, and applying probability to real-world count data. I've been lucky to do research at IIT Goa, IISER Pune, and IISER Thiruvananthapuram, working on everything from Poisson mixture models to community detection in networks.

Outside of research, I'm a Tabla artist, a footballer, and a lifelong fan of Hindustani classical music. On campus I'm the Student Placement Coordinator and the spokesperson for the Music Club. Feel free to look around, and reach out any time — you can find me on LinkedIn, GitHub, or over email.

Unsupervised Feature Selection for scRNA-seq Data

IIT Goa · Under Dr. Clint P. George · May 2026 – Jul 2026

Proposed two novel unsupervised feature selection methods — PLit (Poisson Length Information Test) and ReThiN (Reproducibility via Thinning) — for single-cell RNA-seq data, grounded in information-theoretic and stability-driven principles. Designed and theoretically studied two probabilistic generative models, Admixture Poisson Factor Analysis (APFA) and Geometrically Aggregated Poisson Factor Analysis (GAPFA), and benchmarked against state-of-the-art methods across seven public scRNA-seq datasets.

Bayesian Inference Poisson Factor Analysis Information Theory Feature Selection

Reducing Food Wastage & Procurement Loss — SHODH

IISER Thiruvananthapuram · Under Dr. Zakaria Laskar · Jan 2025 – Apr 2025
